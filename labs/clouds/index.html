How to judge the result

Keep GeoColour at 100% opacity to compare panel 1’s regional features with panel 2’s detailed vector roads and labels. Fade the imagery to see the underlying street map. GeoColour includes its own land and ocean surface; it is not cloud-only.

Panel 3 requests Meteomatics’ sat_cloud_layer:idx only when enabled. Its photographic visible clouds require daylight. The provider may select a nearby observation internally; the requested UTC time is shown, not claimed as a verified acquisition time.

Load once with “Bypass lab cache”, then with “Reuse lab cache”. Bypass affects this lab’s image requests only; upstream servers, network intermediaries, map tiles and catalogue caches may still be warm. It does not erase the app’s cache.

Optional IPMA radar uses its original bounds and the latest available five-minute slot at or before the selected time (up to 30 minutes back). An unavailable radar image is reported independently.

Sources: EUMETSAT EUMETView, OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap, and optional Meteomatics. Provider access and distribution permissions depend on your account. This lab does not change the production app.
