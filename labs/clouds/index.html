How to judge the result

Keep GeoColour at 100% opacity and use the map-feature switches to compare geographic context without fading the clouds. In the masked comparison, clear land and water should expose the street map while clouds retain their photographic shading. Look for missing thin clouds, hard mask edges and coastlines that move when you fade the imagery. Compare Low and High at the same UTC time.

Natural Colour depends on sunlight. Test daylight, sunset and night separately; a dark nighttime result is not a successful photographic nighttime layer. A categorical mask cannot reproduce physical partial cloud transparency. Exact mode omits mixed palette pixels. Palette-blend mode reconstructs their cloud contribution from the documented class colours, preserving resampled edges; it does not estimate cloud optical thickness.

Load once with “Bypass lab cache”, then with “Reuse lab cache”. Bypass affects this lab’s image requests only; upstream servers, network intermediaries, map tiles and catalogue caches may still be warm. It does not erase the app’s cache.

Optional IPMA radar uses its original bounds and the latest available five-minute slot at or before the selected time (up to 30 minutes back). An unavailable radar image is reported independently.
